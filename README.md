# Booking Assurance: clickable prototype

**Live prototype:** https://klook-booking-assurance.vercel.app/

A clickable mobile prototype of **Booking Assurance**, a proposed feature for the Klook travel app, built for a product management assignment.

When you book an activity, Klook sends your booking details to the operator who runs it, and the operator keeps their own copy. If the two copies differ, travellers usually find out at the check-in desk. Booking Assurance shows the traveller exactly what was sent to the operator, asks the operator to confirm before the activity, and gives the traveller something concrete to show, plus urgent help, if their booking still isn't recognised at the desk.

## Using it

Open the live link on a desktop browser. Tap through the app inside the phone frame, or use the demo bar below the phone to jump straight to any state.
